SAP Share Portfolio — Help

Welcome to SAP Share Portfolio — your personal tracker for SAP share purchases and P&L in INR.

1
Load your CSV
Click Load CSV file in the top bar and select your SAP Share overall.csv. Data saves to your browser automatically — no need to reload next time.
2
Live prices auto-fetch
SAP EUR price and EUR→INR rate load automatically. Click ⟳ Refresh Prices to update. Enable Auto (15 min) for hands-free refresh.
3
Review your portfolio
The tiles at the top show your holdings, current value, unrealised P&L, break-even price, and realised gains. Click the chart tiles to see detailed graphs.
4
Add new purchases
Use Add New Purchase / Award Row to record new lots. The exchange rate toggle lets you enter EUR→INR or INR→EUR.
5
Log a sell
Use Log Sell to record a sale. Lots are consumed oldest-first (FIFO). Capital gain is calculated automatically.
6
Backup your data
Click Export CSV regularly to save a timestamped backup. Your data lives in this browser — clearing browser data will erase it.

Where is my data stored?
Your portfolio data is stored only in this browser's localStorage. Nothing is sent to any server. Clearing your browser data will erase it — export a CSV backup regularly.
Why is SAP price not loading?
The price is cached for 5 minutes. Wait for the countdown on the Refresh button to finish, then click it again. If it still fails, Google/Yahoo may be rate-limiting — try again in a few minutes.
My data disappeared after refresh — why?
Clearing browser history/cache wipes localStorage. Always export a CSV backup before clearing. You can reload from the CSV using Load CSV file.
What is FIFO?
First In, First Out — when you log a sell, the oldest lots are consumed first. This matches the standard accounting method for share sales.
What is Break-even?
The SAP EUR price (at current exchange rate) at which your total portfolio would be exactly at zero P&L. If SAP trades above this, you're in profit.
What is STCG / LTCG?
Short Term Capital Gain (held < 1 year) and Long Term Capital Gain (held ≥ 1 year). These come from your sell transaction records.
Can I use this on mobile?
Yes — the layout is responsive and works on mobile browsers.
How do I share this app?
Visit github.com/prasadjalsa/sapshareprice to find the app.
Can I edit mistakes in my data?
Yes — click ✎ Edit in the Currently Held Shares or Transaction History section headers to enter edit mode. Cells become inputs, click ✔ Save All when done.
